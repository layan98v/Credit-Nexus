# Credit Nexus — Quick start

1. Open the website link — or double-click `CreditNexus.exe` (Mac/Linux: `CreditNexus`). Nothing to install, no signup.
   Windows warning "protected your PC" → *More info → Run anyway*.
2. Type a company (`TCS`, `Reliance Industries`, `AAPL`) → **ANALYZE**.
3. Numbers, score, stress test and warnings appear in seconds. AI commentary follows in 30–90 s (free AI allows one request every 30 s).

Desktop app: keep its black window open; close it to stop. Problems? Click **Health** → **Auto-repair**.
